You should never call the exit function.
exit (without the underscore) performs cleanup actions, 
including messing with open stdio files, 
that shouldn’t happen in child processes (they should only happen in the parent shell). 
If you call exit instead of _exit from child processes, you may see weird behavior where, 
for example, the parent shell re-executes parts of a command file.
